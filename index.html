Highly Capable (HiCap) testing (e.g. Naglieri General Abilities Test & Iowa Assessments) typically evaluates students at one or more grade levels above their current placement. This curriculum scales from early foundations up to 5th-grade elite levels to systematically build cognitive stamina, spatial reasoning, and social-emotional growth as Ada grows.

Verbal: Abstract analogies, complex text structure reading.

SEL: Perspective taking, emotional regulation under pressure.
